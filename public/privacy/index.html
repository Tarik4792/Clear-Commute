Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Overview

ClearCommute is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Commute data: Transit line, origin/destination stations, departure time, and trip purpose you enter to generate crowd forecasts.
  • Community reports: Crowd reports you voluntarily submit.
  • Account information: Email address if you create an account.

How We Use Your Information

  • To generate AI-powered crowd forecasts and commute recommendations.
  • To display aggregated community crowd reports.
  • To save your commutes if you choose to do so.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your information with third parties except as necessary to operate the service.

Data Retention

Commute data is used to generate your forecast and is not stored beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

Children's Privacy

ClearCommute is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
